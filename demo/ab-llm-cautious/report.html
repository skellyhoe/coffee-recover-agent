That makes 10 drafts in total. **What I verified:** `verify_recovery` returned ok with no mismatched days. Nine of the 10 drafts match the vendor data and the Excel export. **Still unverified:** Draft 2026-09-21 (total 19020) is only partly checked. The Excel export lacks ORD-1373 to ORD-1380, which together come to 8060, and the Excel total is 10960. I escalated this. **Escalated, not mapped (11 days still held):** - **SIM-0044-e6a1:** The proposal is Kiln Americano, but only by elimination, which isn't strong enough. It affects 09-08, 09-09, 09-13 and 09-17. - **SIM-0053-c07f:** The evidence conflicts. Its price pairs with both Beacon Flat Brew (3 orders) and Beacon Macchiato (5 orders). Several held days contain more than one unresolved code, so they stay held until every code on them is resolved.
